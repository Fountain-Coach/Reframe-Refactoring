# Accessibility declaration

**Status: 17 August 2026**

The governance book is a free technical publication, not a paid e-book or e-commerce service. The current publisher
classification is not legal advice or a conformity certificate.

FCIS-AX is the semantic authority and FCIS-VRT is the visual authority for this web publication.

- Semantic HTML provides named banner, main, chapter-navigation, headings, footer, skip link, and labeled controls.
- The generated site uses ordinary HTML/CSS and browser controls; no custom canvas is required for the publication.
- The responsive chapter rail exposes its menu state through `aria-expanded` and `data-open`.
- The acceptance runner records accessibility-tree assertions and VRT captures for light, dark, and mobile states.
- The site supports reduced motion and follows the operating system's light/dark appearance preference.

Engineering evidence is not a legal conformity assessment. Production hosting, a future paid/e-book service, or a
material change to the surface requires a fresh accessibility and compliance review.
